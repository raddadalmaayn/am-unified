Sanity: throughput is conserved across the two settings because the change in
block occupancy is compensated by the change in block rate:
  10ms: 7.81 tx/block x 71.97 blocks/s = 562.3
  50ms: 24.39 tx/block x 23.25 blocks/s = 567.1
These products exceed the measured steady TPS because blocks_produced counts
every block in the run, including warm-up and drain, whereas steady TPS excludes
both. The comparison of interest is 10ms against 50ms, not the absolute product.
